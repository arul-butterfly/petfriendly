What We Do

Pet-Friendly Spaces

We advocate for more parks, cafes, and public spaces that welcome our furry friends, making the city enjoyable for everyone.

Animal Welfare

We partner with local shelters to ensure every stray animal gets proper care, medical attention, and a loving home.

Pet Education

We organize workshops and create resources to help pet owners be responsible and give their pets the best life possible.

Impact Stories

Transforming Abandoned Lots into Dog Parks

In collaboration with the city council, we transformed three abandoned lots in the downtown area into beautiful dog parks. What was once neglected space is now a thriving community hub where pets and their owners gather daily.

The parks feature safe fencing, water stations, agility equipment, and plenty of shade. Local businesses have reported increased foot traffic, and the neighborhood has seen a significant decrease in petty crime.

Senior Cats Finding Forever Homes

Our "Golden Whiskers" program has successfully placed over 200 senior cats in loving homes in just one year. By educating the public about the joys of adopting older pets and subsidizing medical costs, we've given these cats a second chance at happiness.

Many adoptive families report that their senior cats have brought immense joy and companionship, especially to elderly residents who benefit from the calm presence of these mature feline friends.
